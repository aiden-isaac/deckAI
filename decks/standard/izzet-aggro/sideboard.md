The present 75 has no Broadside Barrage, so that exact plan cannot be copied. The same article identifies creature interaction as the matchup role. The following is a **test proposal by inference**, not a published or tested exchange:

This exchanges five cards for five and uses only cards in this frozen 75. It trades some top-end spell/pump slots for cheap interaction and tempo.
