# Izzet Prowess pilot profile

This profile accompanies the frozen Charis Kikidis World Championship 32 list dated 2026-11-13. Use it as strategic guidance; the external pilot still chooses every action. The tournament result is evidence that this exact 75 was played, not proof of matchup strength.

## Plan

Build an early threat, then turn cheap noncreature spells into a short, high-damage attack. Preserve enough cards to keep threatening after interaction. Slickshot Show-Off rewards sequencing spells on the turn it attacks; Elusive Otter and Pompous Battlemage provide additional prowess pressure. Source S2 discusses the older versions of these lines; adapting them to this list is inference.

## Mulligans

Prefer an opening hand with an early threat, the mana to deploy it, and at least one cheap spell or cantrip. Mulligan hands made only of spells or only of expensive cards. This is an inference from the current list and the strategy sources; no exact seven-card keep table for this registered 75 was found.

## Sequencing and priority

- Before committing a threat, check whether the hand can protect it or replace it after removal.
- Plot/sequence Slickshot only when the delayed cast enables a stronger spell-heavy attack; hard-cast it when immediate haste damage matters. This principle comes from the pilot guide's card discussion, adapted to the current 75.
- Sequence cantrips and pump around blockers and removal. Recalculate lethal after each spell; do not spend pump merely because mana is available.
- When a spell can be held for the opponent's turn, weigh that interaction against the extra prowess damage from casting it before combat.

## Combat and interaction

Count damage before declaring attackers and again after blocks. Use Burst Lightning and sideboard interaction to buy tempo or remove a blocker only when doing so advances a lethal/racing plan. Do not assume the opponent has a specific card; infer it only from public actions and known deck information.
