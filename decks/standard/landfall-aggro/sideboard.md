## Against Izzet Prowess

The first-person RC guide gives a specific Prowess plan for a different mono-green 75: add Torpor Orb, Surrak, and Pawpatch Formation; trim a Harmonizer, two Hydras, and two Badgermole Cubs. The current Worlds 75 has no Pawpatch or Cub and different removal. Do not represent that old exchange as tested for this list. The current Izzet list's Pompous Battlemage enters prepared through a replacement effect, not an enters-the-battlefield trigger; its other creatures do not present an obvious Torpor Orb target. Do not assume Orb helps this matchup.
